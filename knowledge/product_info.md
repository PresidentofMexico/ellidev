# Product Information

## Overview

Elli Enterprise Platform is a comprehensive business automation and AI assistant solution designed for modern enterprises. Our platform combines workflow automation, AI-powered insights, and seamless integrations with your existing tools.

## Core Features

### Intelligent Workflow Automation
Elli automates repetitive tasks across your business operations. The platform uses pattern-based triggers to identify common workflows and execute them automatically, saving your team hours of manual work each week.

Key capabilities:
- Natural language command processing
- Smart entity extraction from conversations
- Multi-step workflow orchestration
- Error handling and retry logic

### AI-Powered Assistant
Our AI assistant, powered by enterprise-grade LLM technology, provides instant answers to business questions by analyzing your company's data and knowledge base.

Features include:
- Conversational interface via Slack
- Context-aware responses that remember thread history
- Integration with data warehouses and CRM systems
- Semantic search over enterprise documents

### CRM Integration
Elli connects seamlessly with Salesforce to provide smart opportunity management, deal tracking, and sales insights.

Supported operations:
- Opportunity search by company name
- Stage updates with validation
- Pre-filled forms with real-time data
- Intelligent disambiguation for multiple matches
