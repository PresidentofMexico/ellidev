# Sales Playbook

## Overview

This playbook provides our sales team with proven strategies, processes, and best practices for selling the Elli Enterprise Platform. Use this guide to navigate the sales cycle from prospecting to close.

## Sales Methodology

### Discovery Phase

The discovery phase is critical for understanding customer needs and qualifying opportunities. Spend 60-70% of your time listening and 30-40% talking.

**Key Questions to Ask:**
- What business problems are you trying to solve?
- What tools are you currently using for workflow automation?
- How many users would need access to the platform?
- What's your timeline for implementing a solution?
- Who are the key stakeholders in the decision process?

**Qualification Criteria (BANT)**:
- **Budget**: $5,000+ annual software budget
- **Authority**: Access to VP-level decision maker
- **Need**: Clear pain point around manual workflows or data silos
- **Timeline**: Looking to implement within 6 months

### Negotiation Stage

### Pricing Objections
**Objection**: "Your pricing is higher than competitors."

**Response**: "I understand pricing is important. Let's look at total value, not just cost. Our customers see 60% time savings on manual tasks, which typically pays for Elli within 6 months. Plus, our Professional tier includes unlimited workflows while competitors charge per automation. What specific ROI metrics matter most to your team?"

**Objection**: "We need to think about it."

**Response**: "I completely understand. Making the right decision takes time. Can I ask what specific concerns you'd like to address? Is it pricing, technical fit, or implementation timeline? I'm happy to provide additional information or set up a trial period."

### Discovery Stage

**Goal**: Understand the prospect's needs, pain points, and decision-making process.

Key questions to ask:
- What prompted you to look for a solution like Elli?
- What manual processes are consuming the most time?
- How do your teams currently update CRM data?
- What's your timeline for making a decision?
- Who else needs to be involved in the evaluation?

**Common pain points**:
- Sales reps spend 2+ hours/day on manual CRM updates
- Employees can't find information quickly (knowledge scattered across systems)

- Manual, error-prone workflows

**Discovery questions**:
- How much time does your team spend on manual CRM updates?

- How many people need access to this platform?
- What's your current process for [specific use case]?

### Objection Handling

**Objection**: "We already have Salesforce, why do we need this?"
**Response**: "Elli enhances Salesforce by adding natural language interaction and AI-powered assistance. Instead of navigating through multiple Salesforce screens, your reps can update deals directly from Slack in seconds. Think of us as the intelligent interface layer on top of your existing tools."

**Objection**: "This seems expensive for a Slack bot."
**Response**: "Elli is much more than a chatbot. It's a workflow automation platform that saves each user 5-10 hours per week. At $99/month for Professional, that's less than $25 per week. If it saves even 2 hours per week, the ROI is 10x based on average employee cost."

**Objection**: "We already have Salesforce Einstein AI."
**Response**: "That's great! Elli complements Einstein by bringing AI capabilities directly into your team's Slack workflow. Instead of switching to Salesforce, your team can ask questions and update deals right in Slack where they're already working."

### Competitive Intelligence

#### vs. Salesforce Einstein
- **Advantage**: Elli is easier to set up (30 days vs 90+ days)
- **Advantage**: Slack-native interface (no context switching)
- **Disadvantage**: Einstein has deeper Salesforce integration
- **Positioning**: "Elli is for teams that want AI automation without the complexity"

#### vs. Microsoft Power Automate
- **Advantage**: Natural language interface, no coding required
- **Advantage**: AI-powered smart suggestions
- **Disadvantage**: Fewer connectors (60 vs 400+)
- **Response**: "We focus on deep integrations that work out-of-the-box, not breadth"

#### vs. Zapier
- **Elli advantage**: AI-powered, enterprise-focused, deep CRM integration
- **Zapier advantage**: More integrations (5,000+)
- **Positioning**: "We're built for enterprise workflows, not simple triggers"

### Handling Objections

**"Too expensive compared to alternatives"**
- Response: Focus on ROI - show time savings calculations
- Our Professional tier at $99/user saves 10+ hours/month per user
- At $50/hour labor cost, that's $500/month in savings per user
- ROI positive in first month for most customers

**"We already use [competitor]"**
- Acknowledge their current solution
- Focus on differentiation: Our AI is context-aware, our Salesforce integration is deeper
- Offer pilot program: 10 users, 30 days, money-back guarantee

**"It's too expensive"**
- Focus on ROI: 60% time savings, typical payback period of 6 months
- Offer Professional tier trial for 30 days
- Highlight early payment discount (2% off)
